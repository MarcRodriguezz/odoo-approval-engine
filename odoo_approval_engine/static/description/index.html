Approval & Rules Engine

Turn any Odoo action into a configurable, multi-stage approval flow — with no code. Hold confirmations, postings and submissions until the right people say “yes”, with full traceability and a reporting dashboard.

Sales · Purchase · Invoicing · Expenses integrations included.

Stop chasing approvals over email and WhatsApp

Define a rule once — “a sales order over €10,000 needs a manager” — and Odoo enforces it automatically: the action is blocked, routed to the approvers, and only runs when every stage approves. Everything is logged.

What you get

⚙️ Rules in plain language

Pick a document, the exact action to gate (confirm, post, submit…) and a condition (point-and-click domain or one-line Python).

🪜 Multi-stage approvals

Sequential stages, each by a group or expression, with a minimum number of approvals. One member of the group is enough.

📥 One-click inbox

“My Approvals” shows everyone what they can approve — live, by group membership. Approve or reject with a reason in one click.

📊 Reporting dashboard

Graph & pivot: pending by document, by rule, by month, and the average approval time. See where things get stuck.

🧾 Full traceability

Who approved what, when, under which rule — plus chatter and email notifications. Reopen / step back any time.

🌍 Generic & multilingual

Works on any model for on-create / on-modify rules. English & Spanish included. Community-only — no Enterprise needed.
